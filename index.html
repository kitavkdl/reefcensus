Asked to count fish. It also found the animals nobody asked for.

SAM 3.1 only sees what it is told to look for. Muse Spark looks first & names what is there in words SAM can act on, then checks SAM’s work frame by frame. Four 12-second clips of deep-sea baited-camera video with every model answer computed beforehand. Click a clip to see the counts & the evidence & the review queue.

On another dataset (24 held-out luderick fish videos not used in development) hand-counted separately by two people, the pipeline’s MaxN is closer to the hand count than the human reference boxes are: summed error 6 against 16 for the first count (21 of 24 counted blind) and 2 against 14 for a second person who never saw the project’s output (exact on 22 of 24); within one fish on all 24. Of 40 randomly drawn boxes the reference called false none was judged not a fish. Reviewing the pipeline’s extra tracks on a phone, one person added 145 missing fish boxes to the reference in 9 min 39 s.

Every model answer is precomputed so there is nothing to install; answer the review queue right here. Run your own clips & read the paper: GitHub
